Formula

M = P · r / (1 − (1 + r)−n)
P = loan amount
r = annual rate ÷ 12
n = years × 12

Worked example

$300,000 at 6.5% for 30 years: r = 0.005417, n = 360, so M = $1,896.20 a month, with $382,633 paid as interest.
