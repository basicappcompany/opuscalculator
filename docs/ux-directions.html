Opus Calculator: three design directions

Mockups for you to choose from, built with the real BMI content so the layouts are judged on real text. Open each tab to see the home page and a calculator page on desktop, and the calculator page on a phone. Nothing here is live, and the counts ("1,000+", "340 calculators") are illustrative of a fully published catalogue: today 5 calculators are live. All three answer the same problems: the tool is too far down, the toolbar and buttons compete, there is no search, and the AI and agent side of the site has no face yet.

A · Calm Precision. Light, editorial and quiet: a serif display face, lots of air, one accent, an oversized result. Calculator first on every screen; the long explanation moves below the tool. A search bar and a single "Use with your AI assistant" band make the new capabilities visible without shouting. Best for trust (health and finance) and for a broad, non-technical audience. Lowest risk, easiest to build on what exists.

D · 23 skins and the current look, all live on the real site. You do not have to choose from pictures: open https://opuscalculator.com/?design on any page and a Design button appears in the header. It lists these 23 skins (plus the current look as "Classic"), three layouts (Standard, Instrument, Focus) and two densities, and applies them to the real calculators, hubs and static pages, in light and dark. Your choice is kept on this device only, and nobody else sees it: visitors get the current look until you decide. Add ?design=off to switch the preview off. Every skin passes the site's contrast rules in both themes (text 4.5 to 1 or better, 7 to 1 for High Contrast). The cards below are generated from the same data the site uses.

The Instrument layout (direction C) is one of the three layouts and works with every skin: inputs and result side by side with the result staying in view, tabular numerals, tighter spacing, and on a phone the result pinned to the bottom edge. The skin called Instrument pairs it with neutral greys and a signal orange.
